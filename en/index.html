Aftercare guide

Half of the result
is made after treatment.

General aftercare for each treatment. Your doctor’s personal instructions always come first.

Do

  • Stay upright for 4 hours after treatment.
  • Gently move the treated muscles a few times in the first hours.
  • Return after two weeks to review the result and touch up if needed.

Avoid

  • No strenuous exercise, sauna or pool for 24 hours.
  • Don’t massage or press on treated areas.
  • Don’t lie down or bend over for 4 hours.
  • No facials or facial massage for 2 days.

Do

  • Use a cold compress wrapped in clean cloth for swelling (10 minutes at a time).
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Sleep with your head slightly raised the first night.
  • Call the clinic immediately if you notice severe pain, blanching or skin discolouration.

Avoid

  • No make-up on treated areas for 24 hours.
  • Avoid sauna, hot baths and strong sun for two weeks.
  • No strenuous exercise for 48 hours.
  • Avoid dental work for two weeks (especially after lip filler).

Do

  • Apply high-SPF sunscreen daily.
  • Use aloe vera gel or a repair cream for any redness.
  • Shave only between sessions.

Avoid

  • No hot water, sauna or pool for 48 hours.
  • No waxing, epilating or threading between sessions.
  • No tanning two weeks before or after a session.
  • No deodorant or perfume on the area for 24 hours.

Do

  • Sleep on your back with your head raised for a week.
  • Eat soft foods and avoid opening your mouth wide.
  • Complete any prescribed medication.

Avoid

  • No facial massage for two weeks.
  • No strenuous exercise, sauna or pool for two weeks.
  • No dental work or heat-based facial treatments for a month.

Do

  • Cleanse with a gentle cleanser.
  • Use sunscreen.
  • Attend every session in your course.

Avoid

  • No make-up for 12 hours.
  • No strenuous exercise or sauna for 24 hours.
  • After scalp PRP, don’t wash your hair for 6 hours.

Do

  • Use sunscreen daily, especially after plasma jet.
  • Drink plenty of water — it helps clearance after HIFU and contouring.
  • Let plasma-jet scabs fall off on their own.

Avoid

  • Don’t pick scabs or peeling skin.
  • No sauna, pool or hot water for a week.
  • No strenuous exercise for 24 hours after carboxytherapy or lipolytics.
  • No make-up over a plasma-jet area until it has healed.

Do

  • Begin gentle washing from day 2–3 as instructed.
  • Sleep on your back on a high pillow for the first nights.
  • Temporary shedding in month one is normal — don’t worry.

Avoid

  • Don’t touch or scratch the grafts for 10 days.
  • No pool, sauna or strenuous exercise for a month.
  • No tight hats for two weeks.
  • Avoid smoking.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions
you usually ask.

Do injections hurt?

Most injections use numbing and ultra-fine needles and feel like a brief sting. Numbing cream or local anaesthetic is used for lip and dermal fillers.

How do I know the product is authentic?

At Nirvana we show you the sealed packaging, authenticity label and batch number before treatment, so you can verify it.

When does Botox work, and how long does it last?

Botox usually starts working within a few days to a week and is complete at around day 14. Results typically last 3–4 months.

How many laser hair removal sessions do I need?

Typically 6–8 sessions, 4–8 weeks apart, depending on skin and hair colour, area and hormones — followed by occasional maintenance.

Can I have injections while pregnant or breastfeeding?

No. Botox, fillers and most aesthetic treatments are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Please tell the doctor before any treatment.

How much do treatments cost?

Cost depends on the area, amount of product and your plan, and is confirmed transparently after examination. For an initial estimate, send us your photos on WhatsApp.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes. Send three clear photos (front and both profiles) on WhatsApp to receive an initial recommendation and estimate.

How can visitors to Mashhad book?

Message us on WhatsApp before your trip with your travel dates, and we’ll plan your treatment around your stay. This website is available in Persian, Arabic and English.

Is the consultation free?

Yes — skin and hair consultations at Nirvana are completely free. You only pay for treatment you agree to go ahead with.

What should I bring to my consultation?

A list of your medications, any allergies, and — if you’ve had injections or cosmetic surgery before — the product name and date.

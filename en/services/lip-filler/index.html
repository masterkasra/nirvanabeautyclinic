About this treatment

Lip filler is designed to create symmetry, define the lip border and add volume that suits your facial shape. Topical anaesthetic and, when appropriate, a cannula keep discomfort and bruising to a minimum.

Ideal for

  • Thin lips
  • Asymmetric lips
  • Lip border definition
  • Vertical lip lines
  • Lifting the mouth corners

Aftercare

Do

  • Use a cold compress wrapped in clean cloth for swelling (10 minutes at a time).
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Sleep with your head slightly raised the first night.
  • Call the clinic immediately if you notice severe pain, blanching or skin discolouration.

Avoid

  • No make-up on treated areas for 24 hours.
  • Avoid sauna, hot baths and strong sun for two weeks.
  • No strenuous exercise for 48 hours.
  • Avoid dental work for two weeks (especially after lip filler).

Common questions

Will my lips look natural?

Yes. Volume is chosen to suit your proportions and can be built up gradually at the touch-up visit.

How long does lip swelling last?

Most swelling settles within 24–48 hours, occasionally up to a week. Cold compresses help.

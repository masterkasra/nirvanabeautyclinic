Hair Transplant in Mashhad: Choosing, Steps and Realistic Expectations

In a hair transplant, follicles that resist loss (usually from the back of the scalp) are moved to thinning areas. Transplanted hairs typically shed within weeks, which is normal, and results are usually seen between months 6 and 12. Results vary from person to person, and the final decision rests on an in-person examination and consultation.
